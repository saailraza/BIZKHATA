Delete your BIZKHATA account

You can delete your account and all its data (shop details, every entry, parties, bills and uploaded slips) at any time. This cannot be undone, so export a backup first (Menu → Backup).

In the app or on the website

  1. Log in to BIZKHATA.
  2. Open the menu (⋮) → Delete account.
  3. Type your password and confirm.

If you cannot log in

Send a message from your registered mobile number to +92 308 4535456 (WhatsApp) or email info@bizeasesolutions.online with your shop name and mobile number. We will delete the account after confirming it is yours.
