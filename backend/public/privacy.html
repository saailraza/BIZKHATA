BIZKHATA – Privacy Policy

Last updated: · BIZEASE SOLUTIONS

What we collect

When you register we store your name, mobile number, shop name and a hashed (not readable) password. While you use the app we store the entries you type: sales, purchases, other income and expenses, udhar (credit) parties and payments, and your stock figure. If you pay your subscription we store the bank payment slip image you upload.

How we use it

Only to run your khata account: show your reports, protect your account (login), manage your free trial and subscription bills, and let our admin approve your payment or password-reset request. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Separation of data

Every shop's entries are kept in a separate database, so one shop's data is never mixed with another shop's.

Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties, except hosting / database providers that store it for us, or when required by law.

Your choices

You can export all your entries to Excel at any time (Menu → Backup). You can delete your account and all its data inside the app (Menu → Delete account) or by following this page.

Security

Connections use HTTPS, passwords are stored hashed, and the login is protected against repeated wrong attempts.
